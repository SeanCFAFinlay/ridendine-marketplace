1. Overview & Database Architecture

RideNDine utilizes a high-availability PostgreSQL 15 relational database hosted on Supabase, enhanced with the PostGIS geographic extension for spatial radius and distance calculations CONFIRMED: supabase/migrations/*.sql & information_schema.tables.

Key Database Statistics

  • Total Operational Tables: 112 public tables (113 including PostGIS spatial_ref_sys) CONFIRMED: information_schema.tables.
  • Database Views: 4 public views (geography_columns, geometry_columns, order_status_events, partner_api_stats) CONFIRMED: information_schema.tables.
  • Migrations: 64 sequential migrations recorded in supabase/migrations/ establishing referential integrity, indexes, and RLS policies CONFIRMED: supabase/migrations.
  • Connection Mechanisms: Direct connection pooling via Node-postgres (pg) on port 5432/6543, and HTTP REST / WebSocket connection via @supabase/supabase-js CONFIRMED: packages/db.

3. Database Table Catalog by Functional Domain

Domain 1: Orders & Fulfillment (15 Tables) CONFIRMED: information_schema.tables

  1. orders: Master order transaction table storing order number, customer ID, storefront ID, status, financial totals (subtotal, tax, tip, delivery fee, service fee, total), payment status, and prep timing.
  2. order_items: Itemized dishes included in an order with quantity, unit price, item ID, and special kitchen instructions.
  3. order_item_modifiers: Selected customizations and options chosen for each order item with incremental add-on pricing.
  4. order_status_history: Audit trail recording exact timestamps and user IDs for every order state transition.
  5. order_pack_checks: Checklists used by kitchen staff and drivers to verify dish packaging accuracy before handoff.
  6. order_exceptions: Escalated incidents (e.g. food late, customer unavailable, damaged item) linked to specific orders.
  7. deliveries: Delivery fulfillment record storing pickup address, delivery address, driver ID, delivery status, and timestamps.
  8. delivery_assignments: Assignment records linking drivers to specific delivery runs.
  9. delivery_tracking_events: Time-series GPS location breadcrumbs and status changes logged during transit.
  10. assignment_attempts: Automated dispatch attempt log tracking 60-second driver offer lifecycles and acceptance status.
  11. kitchen_tickets: Digital kitchen tickets routed to preparation lines.
  12. kitchen_ticket_items: Individual line items assigned to specific prep tickets.
  13. kitchen_queue_entries: Real-time queue positioning for kitchens operating under high order volume.
  14. refund_cases: Formal customer dispute records tracking requested refund amounts, reasons, and Stripe refund IDs.
  15. payout_adjustments: Credit/debit manual adjustments applied to orders, drivers, or chefs following support resolutions.

Domain 2: Kitchens, Chefs & Menus (18 Tables) CONFIRMED: information_schema.tables

  1. chef_profiles: Primary identity for kitchen creators storing legal name, display name, bio, phone, and verification status.
  2. chef_storefronts: Public kitchen brand profiles with slug, cuisine tags, logo, banner image, queue limits, and status.
  3. chef_kitchens: Commercial preparation facility addresses with geographic lat/lng coordinates and inspection records.
  4. chef_availability: Recurring operating schedules defining open/close times per day of week.
  5. chef_delivery_zones: Configured delivery polygons and distance boundaries defining where a kitchen delivers.
  6. chef_documents: Uploaded food safety certificates, business licenses, and government IDs.
  7. chef_payout_accounts: Linked Stripe Express Connect account IDs (acct_*) and verification states.
  8. chef_payouts: Historical records of funds disbursed to chefs with Stripe transfer IDs.
  9. menu_categories: Storefront menu groupings (e.g. Appetizers, Entrees, Desserts, Beverages).
  10. menu_items: Individual dishes with pricing, descriptions, allergen tags, daily volume caps, and 86/sold-out status.
  11. menu_item_options: Modifier groupings (e.g. "Choose your protein", "Spice level") attached to menu items.
  12. menu_item_option_values: Individual choices within an option group with price adjustments.
  13. menu_item_availability: Time-specific availability overrides for seasonal or lunch-only items.
  14. menu_item_packaging: Packaging container specifications mapped to menu items for transport safety.
  15. menu_item_recipe_versions: Versioned recipe links associating menu items with ingredient bill of materials.
  16. kitchen_daily_summaries: End-of-day kitchen operational rollups (total orders, GMV, prep speed, waste count).
  17. kitchen_stations: Prep station definitions (e.g. Cold Prep, Wok Station, Packing).
  18. kitchen_station_assignments: Kitchen staff assignments to specific prep stations.

Domain 3: Drivers, Fleet & Dispatch (14 Tables) CONFIRMED: information_schema.tables

  1. drivers: Fleet courier records storing vehicle type, license number, status (pending, approved, suspended), and ratings.
  2. driver_shifts: Shift work logs tracking clock-in and clock-out timestamps and total hours worked.
  3. driver_presence: Real-time online/offline presence tracking with battery level, network status, and current coordinates.
  4. driver_locations: High-frequency geographic GPS coordinates broadcasted by active drivers.
  5. driver_earnings: Aggregated earnings ledgers recording trip base pay, customer tips, and bonuses.
  6. driver_payouts: Historical bank transfers and weekly Stripe payouts disbursed to drivers.
  7. driver_payout_accounts: Bank routing and Stripe Connect account details for driver disbursements.
  8. driver_documents: Scanned driver's licenses, motor vehicle inspection records, and insurance cards.
  9. driver_vehicles: Vehicle details including make, model, year, license plate, and color.
  10. driver_notification_preferences: Push and SMS notification toggles for dispatch alerts.
  11. instant_payout_requests: On-demand instant cash-out requests initiated by drivers.
  12. delivery_events: Detailed log of delivery state changes and transit exceptions.
  13. sla_timers: Active countdown timers tracking delivery and dispatch SLA thresholds.
  14. service_areas: Geofenced municipal service polygons governing driver coverage areas.

Domain 4: Customers & Loyalty (8 Tables) CONFIRMED: information_schema.tables

  1. customers: Customer accounts storing contact details, preferences, and default payment references.
  2. customer_addresses: Saved delivery locations with street addresses, apartment/buzzer codes, and lat/lng coordinates.
  3. carts: Persistent customer shopping carts tied to user IDs and storefronts.
  4. cart_items: Individual items, quantities, and selected options stored in an active cart.
  5. favorites: Customer-bookmarked chef storefronts and favorite dishes.
  6. loyalty_accounts: Customer reward balances tracking earned loyalty points.
  7. loyalty_transactions: Point movements detailing points earned on orders and points redeemed for discounts.
  8. reviews: Verified customer star ratings (1–5) and written feedback linked to completed orders.

Domain 5: Platform, Settings & Ledger (12 Tables) CONFIRMED: information_schema.tables

  1. platform_users: Internal operational personnel with assigned roles (super_admin, ops_agent, finance_manager, support_agent).
  2. platform_settings: Master operational configuration (commission %, customer fee %, tax %, radius, SLAs, updated_by).
  3. platform_accounts: High-level platform asset accounts for accounting reconciliation.
  4. ledger_entries: Double-entry accounting log recording debit/credit balances across every transaction.
  5. promo_codes: Promotional discount rules with usage limits, minimum spend, discount value, and expiration dates.
  6. promo_code_usages: Audit trail recording which customers redeemed promo codes on specific orders.
  7. referral_codes: Customer referral link identifiers and reward rules.
  8. referral_signups: New customer accounts attributed to peer referral codes.
  9. payout_runs: Batch accounting records tracking weekly scheduled disbursement cycles.
  10. checkout_idempotency_keys: Cryptographic tokens preventing duplicate checkout submissions.
  11. stripe_events_processed: Inbound Stripe webhook event deduplication log.
  12. stripe_reconciliation: Settlement audit reconciling Stripe balance reports against internal ledger entries.

Domain 6: Recipes, Inventory & Kitchen Ops (25 Tables) CONFIRMED: information_schema.tables

  1. recipes: Master recipe cards defining culinary dishes and preparation guides.
  2. recipe_versions: Versioned recipe iterations preserving historical ingredient composition.
  3. recipe_ingredients: Bill of materials listing raw ingredients, units of measure, and portion ratios.
  4. recipe_steps: Step-by-step culinary preparation instructions and temperature standards.
  5. recipe_cost_snapshots: Computed batch costs and food margin percentages.
  6. inventory_items: Raw culinary ingredients tracked in storage with reorder thresholds.
  7. inventory_counts: Physical stock count audits conducted in kitchens.
  8. inventory_count_lines: Individual stock item count variances during physical inventory.
  9. inventory_stock_movements: Stock ledger recording receipts, prep depletion, and adjustments.
  10. inventory_waste_events: Spoiled or discarded inventory logging with root-cause reasons.
  11. inventory_alerts: Automated notifications triggered when ingredient stock dips below reorder points.
  12. storage_locations: Physical kitchen storage zones (e.g. Walk-in Cooler, Dry Storage, Freezer).
  13. suppliers: Vendor directory providing raw culinary ingredients and packaging supplies.
  14. supplier_items: Vendor-specific SKU numbers, minimum order quantities, and unit prices.
  15. supplier_price_history: Historical price fluctuations for raw culinary supplies.
  16. purchase_orders: Commercial ingredient purchase orders submitted to food suppliers.
  17. purchase_order_lines: Line items and negotiated costs on supplier purchase orders.
  18. receiving_batches: Delivered food supplies inspected and checked into kitchen inventory.
  19. packaging_items: Food delivery containers, tamper-evident seals, and branded paper bags.
  20. prep_tasks: Daily kitchen batch preparation tasks assigned to cooks.
  21. prep_task_events: Timestamped task progression logs (started, paused, completed).
  22. production_batches: Scaled batch cooking lots (e.g. 50 liters of broth) with yield counts.
  23. production_batch_inputs: Raw ingredients depleted into a specific production batch.
  24. production_batch_outputs: Finished dishes or portioned components generated by a batch.
  25. kitchen_staff: Kitchen employee directory and contact records.

Domain 7: System, Events & Telemetry (20 Tables) CONFIRMED: information_schema.tables

  1. kitchen_shifts: Kitchen employee shift logs and attendance records.
  2. time_entries: Granular clock-in/out timestamps for kitchen workers.
  3. labor_allocations: Labor cost hours mapped to specific menu preparations.
  4. labor_cost_snapshots: Calculated labor expense rollups per operating day.
  5. pay_periods: Weekly and bi-weekly payroll cycle boundaries.
  6. support_tickets: Customer and kitchen support cases with priority and assigned agent.
  7. system_alerts: Automated operational alerts (SLA breaches, dispatch failures, stock depletions).
  8. notifications: In-app notification queue delivered to users.
  9. audit_logs: Immutable record of administrative mutations with before/after JSON snapshots.
  10. domain_events: Event bus recording application-level domain events for asynchronous handling.
  11. ops_override_logs: Specific audit trail of manual dispatcher status overrides.
  12. ops_processor_runs: Background cron processor execution logs.
  13. admin_notes: Internal administrative notes attached to chefs, drivers, or orders.
  14. storefront_state_changes: Audit history of storefront publication, pause, and suspension events.
  15. push_subscriptions: Web Push API endpoint subscriptions for browser notifications.
  16. api_partners: Registered third-party API integration accounts.
  17. api_partner_keys: Scoped API credentials for external integrations.
  18. partner_webhook_deliveries: Outbound webhook dispatch logs to external partner endpoints.
  19. analytics_events: Client-side telemetry and user interaction event stream.
  20. spatial_ref_sys: PostGIS geographic spatial reference coordinate system table.
